OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 • Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Our Privacy Guarantee

UltraWide Video Fill Pro is built on a strict zero-telemetry, local-only architecture. We do not collect, monitor, transmit, store, or sell any personal information or web browsing data. All video scaling, brightness control, and GPU enhancement operations run 100% locally on your machine.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

UltraWide Video Fill Pro ("the Extension", "we", "our") does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Browsing History: The extension does not record, track, or transmit the websites, videos, or URLs you view.
  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, login, email address, password, or profile information is ever required to use the extension.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: There are no third-party trackers, analytics libraries (e.g., Google Analytics), advertising SDKs, or remote diagnostic beacons embedded in the codebase.

2. Local Device Processing Only

All video transformations, calculations, and shader filters are executed strictly inside your local browser instance:

  • Client-Side CSS Transformations: Aspect ratio fitting and zoom transformations are applied directly to HTML5 <video> DOM nodes using hardware-accelerated CSS.
  • Local GPU Shaders: WebGPU and WebGL2 edge enhancement (Sharpness) and tone mapping (HDR Boost) shaders execute entirely on your graphics processor. Video stream frames are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service.

3. Local Storage Usage

The extension uses your browser's native chrome.storage.local API strictly to save your viewing preferences on your device:

  • Your preferred aspect ratio preset (16:9, 18:9, 21:9, or 32:9).
  • Custom crop and zoom adjustment percentages.
  • Sharpness and HDR Boost filter activation levels.
  • Global brightness setting and optional per-site auto-fill toggles.

These settings remain within your local browser profile and are never synced to any external server.

4. Extension Permissions & Justification

UltraWide Video Fill Pro declares only the minimal permissions required to scale and enhance video playback:

  • <all_urls>: Required to detect and scale HTML5 <video> elements across any website you choose to visit (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, educational sites, media portals). The extension does not read, log, or transmit any web page content, cookies, or user inputs.
  • storage: Required exclusively to persist your display preferences locally on your computer via chrome.storage.local.
  • activeTab & scripting: Used solely to query video dimensions and apply styling transformations when you interact with the popup or trigger keyboard shortcuts.

5. DRM Protection & Streaming Platform Safety

The extension is engineered to fully respect Digital Rights Management (DRM) technologies. On hardware-protected platforms (such as Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, and Disney+ Hotstar), the extension automatically operates in a hardware-safe mode. It applies non-intrusive CSS transformations and bypasses canvas captures to guarantee uninterrupted, black-screen-free playback.

6. Inquiries & Open Source Transparency

UltraWide Video Fill Pro is an open-source project. You can inspect the entire source code, build scripts, and shader implementations directly in our public repository.
